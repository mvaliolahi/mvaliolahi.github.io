2010 → today

Fifteen years of making things work.

I started as a developer in 2010. Somewhere along the way the systems got bigger and the job became people: building teams that ship reliably and enjoy the work. Here's the honest record.

Sheypoor

VP of Engineering

Apr 2024 – Oct 2025 · Tehran

Led engineering, infrastructure, data, and QA for a classifieds platform serving more than five million monthly active users, reporting directly to the CEO. Traffic was sliding when I arrived; we stopped the bleed.

5M+ monthly users 30% faster delivery payment completion +50%
  • Restructured engineering into autonomous teams — higher velocity without new headcount.
  • Directed the response to a 50% traffic decline (deep-linking, push, SEO), stabilising five million monthly users.
  • Prioritised platform health: Core Web Vitals compliance and the revival of a mobile app idle for two years.
  • Established quarterly OKRs, structured Agile practices, and clear mentorship pathways.

Takhfifan

Chief Technology Officer

Nov 2022 – Apr 2024 · Tehran

Held unified responsibility for technology and product at a discount and cashback platform. My rule there: engineering priorities must map to money — revenue, cost, or risk.

launched Takhfifan Payment tech + product, one org
  • Owned technology and product end-to-end, tying every engineering priority to revenue, cost, or risk.
  • Set the strategy for proprietary payment infrastructure and led its launch — reducing dependency on third-party providers.
  • Redesigned the organisation into autonomous, cross-functional squads; higher throughput at the same headcount.
  • Commissioned and governed a technical-debt roadmap — eliminating a 45-second checkout bottleneck was one visible result.

Karnameh

Technical Vice President

Dec 2021 – Dec 2022 · Tehran

Led the technical and organisational separation from Divar — building an independent engineering organisation for a vehicle inspection and leasing platform. Surgery on a living system.

zero service disruption new leasing vertical
  • Directed the full separation of infrastructure, data, and codebases without disrupting the parent company.
  • Built the engineering organisation from scratch: hiring, culture, and engineering practices.
  • Relocated technical operations to an independent headquarters.
  • Championed the vehicle-leasing product from conception through delivery — a new business vertical.
