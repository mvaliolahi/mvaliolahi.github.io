About me

the short version

I'm an engineering executive based in Tehran. Since 2010 I've worked across software, systems, and engineering leadership — developer, lead, CTO, VP — at companies including Sheypoor, Takhfifan, and Karnameh.

What I care about professionally: engineering organisations that ship reliably without heroics; technical decisions that are connected to the business; organisation design treated as part of the architecture, not beside it.

What I write about: the decisions that connect engineering and organisation — documented with their context, trade-offs, and the things I'd do differently.
